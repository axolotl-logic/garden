Design requirements:

* Adding a new note should not decrease the total value.
* Adding a link should result in an increase in value

$$
A \cdot links + B \cdot words=score
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
A &= 100\newline
B &= 1
\end{aligned}
$$

On 2026-06-19, the average note under notes was 98.5 words.
