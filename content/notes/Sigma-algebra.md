A $\sigma$-algebra on a set $X$ is a non empty collection $\Sigma$ with the following properties

* Contains $X$
* Closed under complement - If $A$ is a member, then its compliment is a member
* Closed under countable unions - The union of members is also a member

From this follows
* Contains the empty set
* Countable intersections

$(X,\Sigma)$ is called a measurable space.
