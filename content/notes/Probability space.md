An ordered triple of $(\Omega,\mathcal{F},P)$

$\Omega$ - sample space (set of outcomes)
$\mathcal{F}$ - $\sigma$-algebra ([[Sigma-algebra]]) on that sample space
$P$ - [[probability measure|probability measure]] on that $\sigma$-algebra 

This combined with the [[3 axioms of probability theory]]
