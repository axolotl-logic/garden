A probability distribution maps events to their probabilities.  Formally, it is a [[probability measure]]. It is induced by a [[random variable]].

With *discrete probability distributions*, a single outcome corresponds to a single probability. However, with *absolutely continuous probability distributions*, often a single value has zero probability, requiring us to discuss intervals instead of single points.
