Random variables glue real world phenomenon onto probability theory. It maps individual outcomes to something measurable. Put more technically, they are a function that maps two  measurable spaces. 

In the case of statistics, the two spaces are the [[Probability space]][^1] and the $\mathbb{R}$eal numbers. For example, if you have 1d4, the random variable is the mapping of each face of the die to each of $\{1, 2, 3, 4\}$. From this, the [[probability measure]] is now able to give probabilities for real world phenomenon. 


[^1]: The probability function is not needed as part of this definition. It is however needed for it to make sense.
