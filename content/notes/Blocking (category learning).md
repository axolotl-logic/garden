When teaching or independently studying categories (such as artists, birds, and molecules) , blocking is focusing for an extendes period category as opposed to rotating them ([[Interleaving (category learning)|interleaving]]) during a [[interleaving of lengthy tasks is unsupported|single]] learning session. When studying chess tactics this could look like focusing on knight forks, then queen forks, then rook forks and then ending the learning session. Compared to interleaving which would spend less time on each category and then circle back around to knight forks.[^1].

[^1]: As to whether or not either is a good idea, see [[Blocking doubles, interleaving splits]] and walk away with more questions.
