To enter commands, type them into the console window that is launched when you launch Stardew. When you launch Stardew with SMAPI, SMAPI runs in a console window. You'll see it with the log output. Type commands directly in there, then hit enter.

>[!tip] Run the `help` command to see all commands

>[!tip] If there's output while you are typing...
>SMAPI will write messages to the console even when you're typing, pushing the words you wrote out of visibility. You can ignore this and continue on memory. When you hit enter, whatever and only whatever you typed since last time you hit enter will be sent to SMAPI.
>
>Don't remember what you wrote? Clear what you wrote by hitting backspace a bunch and then hitting enter.

For more information and a complete list of commands, see [Modding:Console commands, Stardew Valley Wiki](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Modding:Console_commands).
