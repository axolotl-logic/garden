Getting thoughts or difficult concepts onto a page in order to free up working memory for deeper analysis.
=======
Cognitive offloading is the intentional reduction of cognitive demand. This can be assisted by physical devices such as pens and typewriters. Try using a pen and paper as an extension of your brain, writing down keywords and drawing connections. This will reduce the demand of holding onto those concepts and connections in working memory.
