Germane load is one of the [[3 kinds of cognitive load]] in Cognitive Load Theory. This load is from processes beneficial to schema construction that are not [[Intrinsic load (Cognitive Load Theory)|intrinsic]] to it. For example, the processes of abstraction and elaboration have a benefit, but they are not tied to the elements needed to learn. 

Note that what may produce germane load for some, may become [[Extraneous load (Cognitive Load Theory)]] via the [[Expertise reversal effect]].
