To interleave what is taught is to rotate it. In interleaving, you spend less time on each topic, but revisit them in a [[interleaving of lengthy tasks is unsupported|single session]]. Put fancily, given category A, B, C, interleaving is teaching them in the order ABCABC instead of AABBCC.

It is established that interleaving is beneficial for learning outcomes. The mechanism of *how* it works is a matter of discussions. It seems like the spacing effect alone [[interleaving and the spacing effect|is insufficient as an explanation]]. One explanation is that interleaving directs your attention in a way that is beneficial. For example, differences and similarities may be discovered that would otherwise blend into the background.

When interleaving is studied, it is compared to [[Blocking (category learning)|blocking]]. This is because blocking is the logical opposite. In other words, if it's not interleaving, it's blocking.
