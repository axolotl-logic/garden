Interleaving teaches learning concepts A, B, C in the order of ABCABC, whereas blocking puts them in the order AABBCC.

The benefits of interleaving are clear from the research. One possible exception may be high within-category variability (e.g. identify art eras, see [[identifying artists given their art]]) might benefit from blocking, low within-category variability (e.g. identifying artists) benefit from [[Interleaving (category learning)|interleaving]].[^1] Others, such as MacKendrick in 2015, show no advantage of blocking. As a side note, in this last study the condition of simultaneous "low-between similarity" and "low-within similarity" had people score too low to adequately test if there was an effect in that specific case.


[^1]: Firth, Jonathan, et al. “A Systematic Review of Interleaving as a Concept Learning Strategy.” _Revista De Educacion_, 2021, [https://doi.org/10.1002/REV3.3266](https://doi.org/10.1002/REV3.3266).
