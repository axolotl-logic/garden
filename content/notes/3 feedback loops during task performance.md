We might divide the performance of a task into many looping interactions. These loops encapsulate the feedback mechanism between person, environment, and task. Each of these can further be broken down to create far more than 3 loops. This builds on [[The Behavior Change Wheel (Michie, Susan, et al 2011)]].

The basic 3 loops are as follows.

1. Task effects person, person effects task
2. Environment effects person, person effects environment
3. Task effects environment, environment effects task

Person might be broken down into
1. Physiologically
2. Psychological

Environment might be broken down into:
1. Physical
2. Social

We now have "physical environment effects a person psychology" but how does a person's psychology effect their environment? We introduce *behavior* and rely on transitivity.  

1. Physical environment effects a person psychology
2. A person's psychology effects their *behavior*
3. The *behavior* effects the physical environment
