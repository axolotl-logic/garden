Some frameworks are built of components that are chosen not because they represent something existent, but because they serve a use. Empirical proof of a useful lie is measured by outcomes from telling that lie and not its inherit truth. 

For example, the [[3 kinds of cognitive load]] has been argued to be derived by reason alone and not built following observed effects. See [[The Evolution of Cognitive Load Theory (Qrru, Giuliano, Longo 2019)]] for a discussion regarding this and an exploration of its validity.
