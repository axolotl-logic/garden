Germane load is one of the [[3 kinds of cognitive load]] in [[Cognitive Load Theory]]. This load is from processes beneficial to schema construction that are not [[intrinsic cognitive load|intrinsic]] to it. For example, the processes of abstraction and elaboration have a benefit, but they are not tied to the elements needed to learn. 

Note that what may produce germane load for some, may become [[extraneous cognitive load]] via the [[Expertise reversal effect]].
