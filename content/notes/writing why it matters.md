## Formalism

### Who Cares?

$Party_A$ has $PoV_A$. $Party_B$ has $PoV_B$, where $PoV_B \neq PoV_A$ and $PoV_B$ contains the subset of reader's $PoV_C$

From this we derive:

Where do I and reader agree? $PoV_A \cap PoV_C$
Who am I arguing with? ($Party_A$)
What is their point of view? ($PoV_A$)
What is a consequence of their point of view? ($C$)
Who am I speaking through? ($Party_B$)
What is their point of view? ($PoV_B$)
How do the points of view agree? ($PoV_A \cap PoV_B$ )
How do the points of view disagree? ($PoV_A \Delta PoV_B$ )
Why should reader care about the disagreement? ($PoV$)
