A graph is considered planar if it can be laid out on a 2d plane without edges crossing 

[[Three Utilities Problem (Graph Theory)]] asks if the "utility graph" is planar.

Subgraphs of planar graphs are themselves planar
Supergraphs of a non planar graphs are themselves nonplanar

Regions of a planner graph are known as "faces".
