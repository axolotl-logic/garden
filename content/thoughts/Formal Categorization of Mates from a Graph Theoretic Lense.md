## Terms and Definitions

### Chessgraph

A $Chessgraph$ is the ordered set $\{board, turn, ep, rights\}$  where $board$ is a weighted complete digraph of $64$ vertices with integer edge weights constituting of shortest path,  $turn \in \{\text{'w', 'b'}\}$, and $0 >= ep <= 64$.
### Kingbox

The $kingbox$ is the square of a king along with the squares around the king.
