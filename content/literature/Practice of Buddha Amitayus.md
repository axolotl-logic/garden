# Supplication

To the root and lineage gurus, embodiments of all blessings,
To the bhagavan, longevity deity, bestowing the supreme accomplishment,
To the dakinis and dharma protectors who defeat all adversity,
I supplicate with a one pointed mind of longing.
With compassion, please guide me and all the infinite beings,
to the state without death.
(3x)

# Praise

To all those who are worthy of praise,
I prostrate with bodies
As numerous as atoms in this universe
And praise them continuously with supreme faith.
_Accumulation of merit by reciting three times:_
I take refuge in the Three Jewels,
Confess each non-virtue and misdeed,
Rejoice in the virtues of beings,
Hold on to buddha-awakening in my mind,
In the Buddha, Dharma, and supreme assembly,
I take refuge till awakening is reached.
To fully accomplish the aims of self and others,
I shall arouse the mind of awakening.
Having aroused the mind of supreme awakening,
I invite all beings to be my guests.
I shall practice the delightful supreme conduct of bodhisattvas.
May I become a buddha for the benefit of beings.
(3x)
The fields of accumulation dissolve into me.
_Thus imagine.
Purify with the mantra_
OM SVABHAVA SHUDDHA SARVA DHARMA SVABHAVA SHUDDHO HAM.

# Generation Stage

All grasping at substantiality including conceptual fixation upon self and others is purified into
emptiness.
From that state arises a multi-colored lotus and moon disc seat.
Upon that, both I and the torma appear as the red syllable HRI with long vowel and visarga (ཧྲ
◌ཱིཿ).


From this light radiates, accomplishing the two aims.
The light then returns and dissolves into the HRI syllables
which fully transform into the protector Amitayus.
He is red in color, appearing, yet without inherent nature.
Adorned by all the major and minor marks of the Buddha,
he has one face and two hands. His hair is in a top-knot.
He is beautified by a light-radiating jewel crown, earrings, short necklace, long necklace,
bracelets, and anklets,
along with multicolored silk upper and lower garments.
His hands are in the equipoise mudra holding the vase
filled with the nectar of immortality,
with top ornament and ribbons around the neck.
He is sitting in the vajra-posture.
From the syllables OM at the forehead, AH at the throat, and HUNG at the heart
Center,
light radiates inviting the wisdom beings, identical to the visualized ones.
DZA HUNG BAM HO. They merge inseparably.
Once again, light emanates from the seed syllable at the heart,
inviting the empowerment deities of the five buddha families.
_Offer with_ OM PANCHAKULA SAPARIVARA ARGHAM PRATITSA SVAHA. PADYAM,
PUSHPAM, DHUPAM, ALOKAM, GANDHAM, NAIVIDYAM, SHABDA
(Mudras, bell and damaru)
Thus, the empowerment deities are requested to grant the empowerment.
Thus, they bestow the empowerment by means of water from the vase,
fill up the body, and purify the defilements.
The overflowing water transforms into Amitabha ornamenting the head.
The empowerment deities dissolve into me.
Offer with OM SARWA TATHAGATA AYUR JNANA SAPARIWARA ARGHAM PRATITSA
SVAHA. PADYAM, PUSHPAM, DHUPAM, ALOKAM, GANDHAM, NAIVIDYAM, SHABDA
(Mudras, bell and damaru)
Amitayus, chief guide of this world,
Glorious subduer of all untimely death,
Refuge for helpless suffering beings,


I prostrate to Buddha Boundless Life.
To all those who are worthy of praise,
I prostrate with bodies
As numerous as atoms in this universe
And praise them continuously with supreme faith.
