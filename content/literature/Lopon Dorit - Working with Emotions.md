## Vairochana

### Contemplation questions

• Where in my life do I avoid fully seeing what is happening?
• What happens when I simply allow experience to reflect itself, without
commentary?
• Can I rest in awareness without trying to improve it?
### Everyday embodiment

• Pausing before reacting
• Letting situations reveal themselves instead of categorizing them
• Allowing clarity to emerge naturally

### Vows / Commitments

• Never abandon the view that all phenomena are primordially pure
• Do not reify self, deity, or mandala as truly existent
• Maintain confidence in emptiness inseparable from luminosity
• Never denigrate the Vajrayāna view as inferior or symbolic only
• Do not reduce tantra to mere ritual without realization

### Samaya Broken When:

• One solidifies deity, mantra, or mandala as inherently real
• One rejects emptiness or Dzogchen/Mahamudra view after receiving it

## Akshobhya

### Contemplation questions

• What situations provoke immediate defensiveness in me?
• Can I stay present with intensity without acting it out?
• What remains when I do not move toward or away from experience?

### Everyday embodiment

• Staying grounded during conflict
• Responding rather than reacting
• Trusting stillness in emotionally charged moments

## Ratnasambhava
### Contemplation questions

• Where do I measure myself against others?
• Can I sense inherent worth without comparison?
• What does generosity feel like when it comes from fullness?
### Everyday embodiment

• Natural generosity
• Comfort with receiving and giving
• Feeling “enough” as you are dedicating the Merit

## Amitabha

### Contemplation questions

• Where does desire turn into grasping in my life?
• Can I stay emotionally open without losing clarity?
• What does love feels like when it does not need to hold?
### Everyday embodiment

• Deep listening
• Appreciating beauty without fixation
• Emotional intimacy with boundaries

### Vows / Commitments

• Transform desire into wisdom, not repress nor indulge it
• Maintain sacred outlook toward form, sound, taste, touch
• Do not fall into ordinary attachment or puritanical rejection
• Honor the symbolic and actual consort principle
• Keep mantra and visualization alive with devotion and joy

### Samaya Broken When

• One indulges desire as ego-grasping
• One rejects embodiment, beauty, or bliss as “non-spiritual”
• One demeans the feminine, dākinī, or consort principle
