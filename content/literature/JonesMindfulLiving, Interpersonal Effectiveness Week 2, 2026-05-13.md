DEAR MAN is a recipe that allows you to avoid falling into to extremes when asking for something or saying no. Its goal is maximize the chance of getting what one wants while minimizing harmful behaviors.

The mindfulness exercise this session is to envision scenario where you're making a request. This should only be of mild-moderate intensity. You imagine making the request confidently and kindly.

Know which goals you want your communication to work towards. This will keep you on track, weathering strong emotions, and to choose the right skills. 

**Objectives Effectiveness** - Getting someone to do something, saying no, resolving conflict, getting taken seriously.

* What are the desired outcomes?
* How will you achieve that outcome?

**Relationship Effectiveness** - Fostering relationship

Balance short-term, long-term. Do I need to insist on being right? Nurture relationships.

* How do I want the other person to feel about me?
* How do I maintain this relationship

When balancing needs,there are two extremes. In the first you undercut your needs, the second you undercut the needs of others.

**Self-Respect Effectiveness** - Maintaining how you feel about yourself

* How do I need to feel about myself?
* What do I need to feel that way?

## Objectives Effectiveness

Potential outcomes:
* Rights met
* Action taken
* Action stopped
* Taken seriously

Factor in *their* reality
* What is the person capable of?
* How might they respond?

Ineffective:
* Aggressive
* Indirect
* Bring up past things
* Threats/attacks

### DEAR MAN

For asking or saying no.

**D**escribe - Describe the current situation, what you're responding to. Keep with factual statements.

**E**xpress - I feel/think/want, inward not outward focus. Should be about how *you* feel not how *they* feel.

**A**ssert - Asking or saying no. Be specific.

**R**einforce - Explain positive and negative consequences.

**M**indful - Remain focused on the issue

**A**ppear Confident - confident without being aggressive

**N**egotiate - Give to get
