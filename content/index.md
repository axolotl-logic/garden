## What is this?

This site[^1]  is a garden of interconnected notes and I am its loving gardener. I tare out weeds and plant in the bare patches. 

However, the quality varies as, at their heart, these notes are meant to grow over time before flowering. My watchword is patience so that I may take immediate action.

## Who am I?

I am a recovering software engineer who loves to learn and share. My mission is to benefit others and help forward humanity's collective creativity, wisdom, and technique. I am a spicy queer and proud cat guardian.

This site is meant to be casually walked through like a royal garden. There are hidden hashtags and notes not exposed by the explorer. See what you can find! To get started, checkout the explorer on the left for the top 3 tags of each of the root tags. Choose your own adventure.
