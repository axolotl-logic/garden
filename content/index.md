# Behold the Axolotl of Logic


So **what is this site?** It is a linked collection of my notes, maintained and elaborated over time. I attend to do it like a loving gardener planting and pruning. Or, perhaps, a bird constructing a nest from disparate threads of knowledge.

And **who am I?** I am a recovering software engineer who loves to research. My mission is to benefit others and help forward humanity's collective creativity, wisdom, and competence. 

In the two decades I spent in tech, I identified as a generalist. As I aged in my career I came to regret this direction, or more to the point, lack thereof. While it meant I understood two layers above and below the code I worked on, it also meant there was no single layer I knew a *lot* about.

The winds are changing and I no longer find software engineering rewarding in a vacuum. I am maintaining this site for the sake of learning what exactly I care about, while also bringing the world around for the journey.
